# Friend Groups

Organize your RuneLite friends list into named, color-coded groups — perfect for
keeping your clan, your ironman buddies, and your PvM team separate at a glance.

## The side panel

Open the **Friend Groups** icon in the RuneLite sidebar to manage everything:

- **New Group** — press the **+ New Group** button at the top to create a group.
- **Rename / delete** — use the pencil and trash icons on each group's header.
- **Recolor** — click the little color square next to a group to pick its color.
- **Reorder** — drag a group by its grip handle to change the order groups appear in.
- **Collapse** — click a group's name to fold it away when you don't need it.
- **Add friends** — press the **+** next to any friend to drop them into a group,
  or remove them with the trash button.

Under each group you'll see its friends, with **online friends first** and their
current world shown alongside. Green means they're on your world, yellow means
they're online elsewhere, and greyed-out means offline. Friends who aren't in any
group appear under **Ungrouped**.

**Double-click an online friend** in the panel to hop straight to their world.

## In the in-game friends list

Your groups can also show up directly in the game's friends list. Right‑click any
friend and choose **Assign group** to add them to a group, remove them, or create a
new one on the spot.

## Settings

- **Side Panel Priority** — where the Friend Groups icon sits in the RuneLite
  sidebar. A lower number moves it higher up.
- **In-game marker** — how your groups appear in the in-game friends list:
  - `Grouped list` (default) — clusters your friends under a colored header for each group.
  - `Coloured dots` — a small color dot per group next to each name.
  - `Off` — no in-game marking.
- **Hover tooltip** — show a friend's group names when you hover over them in-game.
- **Hide offline friends** — hide offline friends from both the side panel and the
  in-game friends list.
- **Hide "World" prefix** — show just the world number (e.g. `369`) instead of
  `World 369` in the in-game friends list.
